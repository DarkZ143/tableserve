## Brand & Style
This design system defines a refined, tactile, and sensory-driven digital dining experience. Designed for upscale dining establishments and luxury hospitality environments, it translates the warmth of linen tablecloths, warm candlelight, and artisanal culinary presentation into a luminous digital interface. The emotional response is one of indulgence, serenity, effortless hospitality, and understated wealth.

The design movement combines **Modern Luxury Editorial** with **Soft Glossy Neomorphism**:
- Rich, luminous ivory surfaces layered over subtle vanilla washes.
- Micro-glassmorphic highlights reflecting light along delicate structural borders.
- Editorial high-contrast serif typography paired with crisp, functional geometric sans-serif numbers and telemetry.
- Tactile, culinary-focused micro-details: dietary markers executed as jewelry-like icons, ribbon badges reminiscent of bespoke wax seals or gilded menu stamps, and fluid, natural transitions.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly for light mode, drawing from high-end pastry, vintage cellar vintages, and organic ingredients:

- **Primary (`#850D32` - Berry Burgundy):** Anchors core actions, bottom checkout sheets, active tab highlights, and primary CTAs. Rich and appetizing, providing commanding contrast against ivory surfaces.
- **Interactive State (`#B21E46` - Raspberry):** Used for hover, press, and active micro-interactions of primary components.
- **Secondary (`#DA9A32` - Honey Gold):** Accents signature dishes, Michelin/sommelier selections, chef’s tasting notes, star ratings, and bestseller ribbons.
- **Tertiary (`#73936D` - Herb Green):** Reserved exclusively for vegetarian culinary icons, dietary certifications, positive balance/success confirmations, and prep-time badges.
- **Neutral Core (`#6A3B2D` - Muted Cacao):** Replaces harsh standard blacks and slate grays for all primary typography, icons, and non-vegetarian dietary insignias.
- **Canvas Base (`#FFF9F2` - Warm Ivory):** The unyielding foundation canvas across all viewports.
- **Card Surface (`#FFF0E3` - Vanilla Cream):** Slightly deeper than the canvas, creating subtle organic volume without relying on heavy borders or jarring fills.
- **Accent Highlight (`#FAD9C8` - Soft Peach):** Tints for badge backdrops, tag pills, selected counter increments, and glass reflection gradients.

## Typography
Typography is orchestrated around a dialogue between classical hospitality literature and hyper-precise modern transaction interfaces:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Set in `Playfair Display`. It communicates pedigree, gastronomic depth, and curated luxury. Used for restaurant names, course categories, signature dish names, and narrative chef stories.
- **Body, UI Labels & Numerical Telemetry:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Delivers immediate legibility on handheld devices under ambient table lighting. Its geometric geometry ensures nutritional data, dietary chips, customizations, and operational statuses scan effortlessly.
- **Price Formatting:** Prices must always be prepended with the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) formatted in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold or bold, ensuring the currency glyph retains clean optical alignment with numerals (e.g., `₹1,450`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a fluid responsive column framework crafted for mobile guest ordering (primary) through host/tablet orchestration (secondary):

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Content is oriented vertically with sticky bottom actions and horizontal carousel scrollers for quick course categories.
- **Tablet / In-Store Terminal (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem` and `gutter-md: 1.5rem`. Enables side-by-side menu browsing and live table cart summaries.
- **Desktop / Dining Management (>= 1025px):** 12-column layout centered at a maximum content width of 1280px with `margin-lg: 3.5rem` and `gutter-lg: 2rem`.

Rhythm is maintained through an 8pt base grid. Spacing is intentionally airy around dish photography and typography titles to replicate high-end spatial menu spreads.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation shuns artificial cold drop shadows in favor of **warm, ambient sunlit diffusion** combined with **subtle micro-glassmorphism**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Base background `#FFF9F2`.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Plates):** `#FFF0E3` surface combined with a dual-layer shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(106, 59, 45, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(106, 59, 45, 0.03)`. Edge definition is reinforced with a 1px solid border of `rgba(133, 13, 50, 0.06)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on the top edge to simulate overhead ambient lighting.
- **Level 2 (Modals, Customization Sheets & Dropdowns):** Translucent backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with surface `rgba(255, 240, 227, 0.92)`, bounded by `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(106, 59, 45, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Order Bar / Sticky Actions):** Surface `rgba(255, 249, 242, 0.94)` with top gloss highlight line `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(250, 217, 200, 0.8), transparent)` and heavy soft diffusion: `0 -8px 24px -2px rgba(106, 59, 45, 0.08)`.

## Shapes
The shape language is generous, welcoming, and soft:
- **Card Containers:** Shaped with `rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl` styling (20px to 28px on hero dish cards), invoking the softened profiles of fine ceramic dishware.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Use pill shapes (`rounded-full`) for quick categorical filters, dietary tags, and floating order buttons to maximize thumb ergonomics.
- **Form Controls & Counter Fields:** Uniformly shaped at 12px (`rounded-md` equivalent to 0.75rem), retaining clean structural alignment without becoming overly stark.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Order Button:** Background `#850D32` (Berry Burgundy) with text `#FFF9F2`. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `1rem 1.75rem`. On hover/tap, transitions smoothly to `#B21E46` (Raspberry) with a subtle micro-scale of `1.015`. Includes high-contrast white currency indicators.
- **Secondary / Pairing Suggestion Button:** Background `rgba(250, 217, 200, 0.45)`, border 1px solid `rgba(133, 13, 50, 0.15)`, text `#850D32`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#6A3B2D` text and a subtle bottom accent line appearing on active states.

### Cards (Dish & Course Modules)
- **Dish Tile:** Background `#FFF0E3`, border radius 24px, 1px subtle translucent border `rgba(106, 59, 45, 0.08)`. Features an edge-to-edge or softly padded 1:1 image container with rounded 18px corners.
- **Price & Increment Controls:** Features an elevated segmented pill: a `-` and `+` button in soft cream with bold `#850D32` count values.

### Dietary & Hospitality Badges
- **Vegetarian Symbol:** Strict 16x16px square with 1px border in `#73936D` containing a centered solid `#73936D` circle (6px diameter).
- **Non-Vegetarian Symbol:** Strict 16x16px square with 1px border in `#6A3B2D` containing a centered solid equilateral `#6A3B2D` triangle (6px height).
- **Bestseller / Chef's Special Ribbon:** Delicate badge using `#DA9A32` Honey Gold text on an ivory background with a 1px border in `rgba(218, 154, 50, 0.3)`, optionally styled with a honey-gold left ribbon accent or star icon.

### Form Inputs & Dietary Preference Selectors
- **Input Fields (Special Requests, Table Notes):** Background `#FFF9F2`, border 1px solid `rgba(106, 59, 45, 0.18)`, focus state border `#850D32` with a 3px glow of `rgba(133, 13, 50, 0.08)`. Text rendered in `#6A3B2D`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio checks featuring `#850D32` active fills with an interior `#FFF9F2` dot. Checkboxes utilize a soft rounded 6px square with an herb or burgundy checkmark.

### Floating Cart & Table Status Bar
- Docked at the bottom viewport with safe-area padding. Glassmorphic treatment with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 240, 227, 0.94)`, displaying item counts, active dining table number (e.g., *Table 14*), and subtotal rendered in `price-lg` font tokens.
